# Clarify

Interview the user until the goal, scope, important interactions and acceptance expectations are clear. Ask questions whose prerequisites are settled, with a recommendation; investigate facts yourself.

Research comparable products for useful features and experience, and implementation approaches for feasibility. Feed findings back into questions. Use read-only subagents when available.

For a frontend, ask whether the user has a design.md, preferred style or visual reference. Use it if supplied. Otherwise randomly choose an available design document from https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md, record its source and revision, and keep that choice across sessions. Existing projects retain their design unless a change is requested.

Write the agreed brief and a design.md draft. Distinguish confirmed decisions, suggestions and open questions. Proceed after the user confirms the scope; subsequent routine stages do not need another approval.
