# Clarify

Read the request, existing decisions and relevant project context. Identify the user, problem, smallest useful outcome, boundaries and observable acceptance. Adopt explicit requests and earlier confirmations instead of restarting an interview.

Use [investigation](investigate.md) for facts or feasibility that affect a decision. Ask only the remaining product questions, in small groups whose prerequisites are settled. Offer a recommendation and explain the consequence of the choice. A safe, reversible implementation detail can be a recorded assumption; an unknown that changes the intended product needs the user's decision.

For a frontend, use the existing design or the user's supplied reference. If none exists, ask about a preferred visual direction while investigating. If no preference is supplied, select an accessible reference that fits the product's audience, content and interaction density; [awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) is one optional source. Record why it fits, its source/revision and the adapted design decisions. When external references are unavailable, write an explicit provisional direction from the brief and identify it as your own proposal. Resolve material brand or product choices with the user; preserve the accepted direction across sessions.

Save the agreed brief in the existing plan or product document and, for a frontend, a design.md draft or its established equivalent. Mark confirmed decisions, assumptions and open questions separately.

Ready when the outcome and exclusions are clear, acceptance can be observed, and no unresolved product choice blocks the next increment. Continue to the relevant planning route; subsequent routine stages need no new approval.
